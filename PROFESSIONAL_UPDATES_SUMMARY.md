## Documentation Professional Standards

### **Status Indicator Standards:**
- **[COMPLETE]** - Task or requirement fully satisfied
- **[PENDING]** - Awaiting action or approval
- **[REJECTED]** - Task failed or not approved
- **[CONDITIONAL]** - Requires minor fixes or adjustments

### **Risk Level Standards:**
- **[LOW RISK]** - Minimal impact, easy rollback
- **[MEDIUM]** - Moderate complexity, manageable risk
- **[HIGH]** - Complex features, higher risk level
- **[WARNING]** - Important caution or attention needed

### **Priority Standards:**
- **[RECOMMENDED FIRST]** - Optimal choice for implementation
- **[CRITICAL]** - Essential for functionality
- **[OPTIONAL]** - Nice-to-have enhancement
